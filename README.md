**Overview**
An interactive Power BI dashboard built on the AdventureWorks Bike Shop dataset to monitor and analyze key business metrics including revenue, profit, orders, and product performance across categories and time periods.
Key Metrics
MetricValue Total Revenue$24.91M Total Profit$10.46M Total Orders25.16K Return Rate2%

Dashboard Features
KPI Cards — High level snapshot of revenue, profit, orders and return rate at a glance
Weekly Revenue Trend — Line chart tracking revenue from Jan 2020 to Jul 2022 with a forecast trendline showing consistent growth
Total Orders by Category — Accessories lead with 16.98K orders, followed by Bikes (13.93K) and Clothing (6.98K)
Top 10 Products Table — Breakdown of best performing products by total orders, revenue and return rate. Water Bottle 30oz ranks #1 with 3,983 orders
Quarterly KPIs — Revenue, Orders, Profit and Returns tracked against goals with % achievement indicators
Most Ordered Category — Tires and Tubes
Most Returned Category — Shorts

Tools Used

Power BI Desktop
DAX (measures and calculated columns)
Power Query (data transformation)
